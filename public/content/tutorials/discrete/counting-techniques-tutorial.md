# Counting Techniques Tutorial

This tutorial covers fundamental counting principles and techniques used in discrete mathematics.

## Basic Counting Principles

### Addition Principle
If there are n ways to do one thing and m ways to do another, and the two actions are mutually exclusive, then there are n + m ways to choose.

### Multiplication Principle
If there are n ways to do one thing and m ways to do another, then there are n × m ways to do both.

## Permutations

### Permutations of n distinct objects
$$P(n) = n!$$

### Permutations of n objects taken r at a time
$$P(n, r) = \frac{n!}{(n - r)!}$$

## Combinations

### Combinations of n objects taken r at a time
$$C(n, r) = \binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n - r)!}$$

## Properties of Binomial Coefficients

$$\binom{n}{k} = \binom{n}{n-k}$$

$$\binom{n}{0} = 1$$

$$\binom{n}{1} = n$$

Pascal's Identity: $$\binom{n}{k} = \binom{n-1}{k-1} + \binom{n-1}{k}$$
